# Lexius v4

> The update that changed everything

## Here's What's New:

Lexius v4 is finally here!

This update brings a fresh experience to Lexius, with a redesigned interface, improved usability, and a few things that make the editor feel a lot more alive.

### 🎨 New UI

Lexius v4 comes with a completely refreshed UI.

The goal wasn't to make things complicated — it was to make Lexius feel cleaner, more polished, and more like a proper development environment.

A new version deserves a new look.

### ✨ Better UX

The UI isn't the only thing that changed.

We've also worked on making Lexius easier and nicer to use. Small improvements throughout the experience add up to make working with Lexius feel smoother.

Because good UX isn't about adding more buttons.

It's about making the things you already have feel right.

### 🔔 New Notification System

Lexius now has a new notification system!

And this isn't just for V4 announcements.

The system is designed to be reusable, meaning Lexius can use it for future updates, announcements, insights, and other useful information without needing an entirely new interface every time.

Basically, this little blog you're reading right now is powered by it.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

Thanks to AI ([Goose](https://block.github.io/goose/)), I was able to track down and fix several major bugs.

A lot of these bugs were the kind that are incredibly annoying to find — everything looks fine until that one thing randomly decides to explode.

Goose helped me investigate the issues, narrow down their causes, and get them fixed.

So yeah, V4 isn't just prettier.

**It's also less broken.** 💀

### 📰 Insights on Updates

From now on, Lexius can give you more than just a version number.

Instead of simply saying:

> "Lexius v4.0.0 is out."

we can actually tell you **what changed, why it matters, and what's coming next.**

That means updates can be a little more interesting than reading a changelog.

## And That's V4!

That's pretty much what this update is about.

A new UI, better UX, the return of Indu, a new notification system, and a better way to keep you informed about what's happening with Lexius.

It's not the biggest list of features in the world.

But sometimes, a good update isn't about adding a hundred things.

It's about making the things you already have **better**.

Welcome to **Lexius v4**. 🚀
